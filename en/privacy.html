Privacy policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

This is a translation. If there is any discrepancy, the Spanish version prevails.

In short: Pregúntame has no accounts and no sign-up. Your settings, favourites and notes are stored only on your phone. We only receive what you choose to send us (suggestions and reports, without your name). If the app shows ads, Google manages them and asks for your permission to personalise them. We do not sell your data.

1. Who is responsible

The controller of your data is:

  • Owner: Lier Quesada Bayón
  • Location: Durango (Bizkaia), España
  • Email: preguntame.team@gmail.com

The owner's full details are in the legal notice.

This policy applies to the Pregúntame Android app (package preguntame.app) and to this website.

2. Data that stays on your phone

The app stores the following in your device's memory, without sending it to any server:

  • Your settings: language, theme, sound, vibration and reminders.
  • The questions you mark as favourites.
  • The notes you write in the daily personal reflection.
  • A copy of the questions so the app works offline.

We cannot see this data. It is deleted if you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Notifications: if you turn on reminders, the app schedules them on your own phone. To do this it asks for notification permission, which you can withdraw at any time in Android settings. We do not use notification servers.

3. Suggestions and reports you send us

If you use “Send us your question”, “Send a suggestion” or “Report”, we receive:

  • The text you write and the section or reason you choose.
  • For a report, the reported question and where it appears in the app.
  • The app language, the system (Android) and the date and time of sending.

We do not ask for your name or email. Please do not include personal data about yourself or others in the text. Messages are stored in a private Google spreadsheet (Google Workspace) that only the owner can access; the owner also receives an email alert.

Purpose: to review proposals and fix mistakes. Legal basis: your consent when you send the message (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Retention: as long as needed to review it, and at most 2 years.

If our inbox cannot be reached, the app may open your email app so you can send it by email. In that case we will also receive your email address, which we will only use to reply to you.

4. Question updates

To keep the questions up to date, the app regularly downloads a file with the questions from our web server (hosted on Vercel). As with any internet connection, the server receives technical data such as your IP address and device type, which the provider keeps in security logs for a limited time. We do not use it to identify you. Legal basis: legitimate interest in the app working and being secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Advertising

The free version of the app may show ads from Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). To show and measure them, Google may process your device's advertising ID, your IP address, device data and how you interact with ads.

  • When you first open the app, we will show you a consent form. If you accept, ads may be personalised; if not, you will see non-personalised ads, which still use some data to limit frequency, prevent fraud and measure results.
  • You can change your choice at any time in the app's Settings.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Google › Ads).

Legal basis: your consent for personalised ads (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and legitimate interest for non-personalised ads (Art. 6(1)(f)). More information on how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

6. “Remove ads” subscription

The subscription is bought and managed through Google Play. Google processes your payment data; we never see your card. We only receive from Google the information needed to know whether your subscription is active (for example, the status and purchase ID). Legal basis: performance of the subscription contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). See also Google's privacy policy.

7. Who else receives data

We do not sell or share your data. Only these providers process it, as far as needed to provide the service:

  • Google (Google Workspace, AdMob and Google Play).
  • Vercel Inc. (hosting of this website and the questions file).

Some of these providers may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. In that case, transfers are covered by the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

8. Your rights

You can ask us to access, rectify or erase your data, object to or restrict its processing, request portability and withdraw your consent at any time. Write to us at preguntame.team@gmail.com. As we do not ask for your name, to find a specific message please tell us, if you can, the approximate date and the text you sent.

If you think we have not handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD) or to the authority in your country.

9. Minimum age

Pregúntame is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not designed for minors. We do not knowingly collect data from minors. If you think a minor has sent us data, write to us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We apply reasonable measures to protect data: encrypted connections (HTTPS), restricted access to the suggestions inbox and as little data as possible.

11. This website

This website does not use cookies or analytics tools. The hosting provider may log technical data about visits, such as the IP address, for security reasons.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will publish the new version here with its date and, if the change is significant, we will let you know in the app.
